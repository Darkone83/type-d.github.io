Type-D / ESP32 Web Flasher
ESP32 · ESP32-S3 · WebSerial
Browser-based ESP flasher
Auto-detects ESP32 vs ESP32-S3 and flashes merged firmware at 0x0000 and FFat at 0x410000.
Install firmware
Uses firmware/manifest.json for board + layout.
Your browser doesn’t support WebSerial (Chrome / Edge desktop required).
Instructions:
1. Connect your ESP32 / ESP32-S3 over USB.
2. Click “Flash Type-D Firmware” and pick the correct port.
3. Watch the log window for progress and any errors.
Works on Chrome & Edge (desktop) with WebSerial enabled. Source:  github.com/YOURUSER/YOURREPO
